**Notable for cross-jurisdiction consolidation:**

- ★ **Root cause of BC's shortfall is a loader defect, not upstream staleness alone**, and it explains *both* symptoms at once. Open North's BC roster was complete at **93** rows; **41 carry a `NULL` `constituency_id`** and there are **0 orphans**. Zero orphans proves the representative records had **no `related.boundary_url`**, so `_constituency_id()` returned `None`, the politician was written with a NULL key, and the guard at `opennorth.py:590` (`if cid and …`) skipped the boundary fetch entirely — nothing logged. Applies to any jurisdiction whose Open North roster is fresher than its boundary set.
- ⛔ Pagination artefact, regional roster gap, and "roster only covered 52" were each tested and **falsified** — see the dossier's competing-explanations table. 52 is not a page size.
- ★★ **BC publishes a postal→district concordance**: LIMS `allPostalCodeLookups`, **164,848 postcodes, 93/93 current districts, 0/94 historical**, no auth. The A3 exception — usable as a **cross-check on PIP output**, though its licence is unestablished (see licence row).
- ★ **District↔official mapping is exact**: LIMS `allConstituencies` active-93 names equal Elections BC `ED_NAME` **93/93, zero divergence**; `Constituency.memberId` → existing `politicians.lims_member_id`. BC is a strong early-cutover candidate under the authority-column strategy.
- ⚠ **Three silent mis-join traps** (full evidence in the dossier): `ELECTORAL_DISTRICT_ID` generation-scoped (0/52 stable); **8 `ED_ABBREVIATION` codes recycled onto different districts** (`BNN` = Burnaby North in 2015, Burnaby-New Westminster in 2023); and **shapefile `ED_ID` is exactly one less than WFS `ELECTORAL_DISTRICT_ID`, 87/87** — contiguous IDs make that off-by-one map every district onto its alphabetical neighbour silently.
- ✅ **A4 checks clean:** one row per district in both generations (93/93/93 and 87/87/87 distinct IDs — no island fragmentation); no `.cpg` in any case (encoding undeclared, but all names pure ASCII); `.prj` has **no `AUTHORITY` clause** — EPSG:3005 derived by hand from the parameters, and distinguished from EPSG:3153 by the `D_North_American_1983` datum string.
- ⛔ **A6 prefix — BC is the acute case.** Current prefix `british-columbia-electoral-districts-2015-redistribution` encodes not just a generation but the **wrong** one (2015 geometry, 2023 names). Target `british-columbia-electoral-districts/<slug>`. ★ With a generation-free prefix the 52 existing rows keep their key, take `effective_to = 2024-09-21`, and the new generation lands as a second `boundaries_version` under the same keys — **the first real exercise of migration `0021`'s temporal model**, and BC is the best place to prove it since both generations are identified and staged. Does **not** soften the all-93-are-new-inserts rule: keeping the key is not keeping the row.
- ★ **Free staleness canary:** the rolling layer `EBC_PROV_ELECTORAL_DIST_SVW` is BS11 under an alias; poll its `BOUNDARY_SET_ID` (currently `11`) with `propertyName=BOUNDARY_SET_ID&count=1` to detect the next redistribution.
